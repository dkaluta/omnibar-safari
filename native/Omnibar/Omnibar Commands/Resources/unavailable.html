Omnibar Commands

Open the palette on a website.

Open a regular webpage, then press ⇧⌘P. The palette appears in the center of that page.

Safari’s Start Page, PDFs, and protected pages can prevent extensions from showing a panel. If this happens on a regular website, check Omnibar Commands in Safari’s extension settings.

Your original tab is still open. You can close this help tab with ⌘W.
